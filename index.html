About

Why AI

Realms

Where the interior
work lives.

The framework beneath everything Spheres & Realms builds.

Most of us move through our days on patterns we didn't consciously choose — giving more than we have, protecting ourselves from things that no longer threaten us, or both.

The Leaking Hero Tracker is an 8-day self-observation practice that brings awareness to where these patterns run — so that choice becomes possible and compensation can be dealt with.

A conversation first. Then one honest entry each day. At day four and day eight, the pattern is reflected back. Not as diagnosis. As a map — built entirely from what you brought.
The framework

The Leaking Hero — cutting ourselves for others

The False Hero — armouring ourselves to override

The Original Self — the action coherent with the moment

These are not types. They are directions of departure — currents that pull a person from their original vision. The tool does not label you. It shows you where and how you leave yourself.
