What it is

Most of us move through our days on patterns we didn't consciously choose — giving more than we have, protecting ourselves from things that no longer threaten us, or both.

The Leaking Hero Tracker brings awareness to where these patterns run — so that choice becomes possible and compensation can be dealt with.

The framework

Three directions of departure.
One original map.

I

The Leaking Hero

Cutting ourselves for others. Giving before asked. Explaining to maintain closeness. The room breathes easier when we are there — but we cannot breathe inside ourselves.

II

The False Hero

Armouring ourselves to override. Productivity as proof. Distance that feels like groundedness. Something real has been built — but the person is not quite inside it.

III

The Original Self

The action coherent with the moment. Not spiritual quietude — can be sharp, direct, even angry. What makes it original is its source: the moment, not the current.

These are not types. They are directions of departure — currents that pull a person from their original vision. The tool does not label you. It shows you where and how you leave yourself.

How the practice works.

1

A conversation first. An AI-guided exchange that surfaces your current pattern and maps it to the framework. Minimum six exchanges before any reflection is offered.

2

Eight days of tracking. One honest entry per day — the most charged moment, observed through a few precise questions.

3

Pattern reflection at day four and day eight. What the tracking showed — the recurring shape of the departure, the current that kept running.

4

A map, not a verdict. Built entirely from what you brought. The more honestly you speak, the more specific it becomes.

Your entries are used only to generate your pattern tracking. They are not read or reviewed by any person. The moment you describe each day is released as soon as your entry is complete. What you share here is between you and the practice.

Begin when you're ready.

There is no right time. There is only the decision to look honestly at what has been running.
